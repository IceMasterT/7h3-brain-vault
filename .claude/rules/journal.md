# Journal (episodic memory)
- One file per day: 60-journal/YYYY-MM-DD.md from _templates/journal.md.
- Append only. One line per entry under Log: "- HH:MM (agent): what happened".
- Never rewrite or delete past lines. Correct a mistake with a new line.
- Learned holds durable lessons. The weekly review promotes them into notes.
- Prefer `brain_journal` or `python3 scripts/brain.py journal "..."`: they stamp time and agent.
